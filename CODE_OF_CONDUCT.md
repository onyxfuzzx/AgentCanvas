# Community code of conduct

AgentCanvas welcomes people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Keep discussion useful, respectful and focused on the project.

We expect contributors and maintainers to:

- Critique ideas and behavior with evidence; respect the people involved.
- Accept corrections, give constructive feedback and respect decisions about scope.
- Protect privacy, especially in screenshots, workspace files and agent output.
- Credit others' work and make room for new contributors.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized content, doxxing, impersonation, spam and repeated disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit/remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions, restrict participation or remove access. They should explain decisions when doing so will not expose private information or increase harm. Maintainer status does not exempt anyone from this policy.

Use GitHub's reporting tools to report abuse. You may ask `@onyxfuzzx` for a private contact channel without naming affected people or sharing private details publicly. Security vulnerabilities use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). We do not promise confidentiality for information posted in public threads or guarantee a response time.
